# Privacy policy: KS Expense Manager

Last updated: 27 November 2026

KS Expense Manager keeps your expenses on your phone and nowhere else.

- **No data collected, no data shared.** The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.
- **No internet.** The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Expenses, categories, budgets, recurring expenses and settings are stored in the app's private storage. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app.
- **Files only when you ask.** A backup, a restore, or a CSV export reads or writes only the file you choose in Android's file picker. Subscriptions you share from KS TV Launcher are read once, when you share them.
- **Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup or CSV files you saved stay where you saved them.
- **Children.** The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

The app is open source: https://github.com/kumasumit/ks-expense-manager. Questions: open an issue in that repository.
